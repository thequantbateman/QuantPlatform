# Prediction-market probability semantics

A binary YES price is displayed as a market-implied probability coordinate. It is an observed contract price, not certainty and not automatically a calibrated physical probability. Fees, spread, liquidity, market rules, risk preferences and participant composition can create wedges.

The UI therefore labels each value `OBSERVED · NOT FORECAST`, carries source and receive time, reports liquidity and volume, and avoids forecast language or recommendations. Learn connects this distinction to risk-neutral pricing and market-price-versus-model-price concepts.
