# Product

TheQuantBateman teaches quantitative finance through progressive disclosure: intuition → mathematics → implementation → market application → desk view. The first release serves students through front-office practitioners without forcing every reader through the same depth.

The character is an original fictional quant. The brand may use 1980s financial editorial atmosphere, tailoring and dry humour, but must not reproduce a real actor, copyrighted film character, dialogue, scene, logo or third-party identity.
