# Content model

`ContentEntry` defines title, slug, description, asset class, category, difficulty, type, prerequisites, relationships, linked labs, tags, author and review date, plus authored teaching sections. MDX-compatible source lives under `content/`; the typed catalog drives search, routes and relationships.

Difficulty is one of `foundation`, `practitioner`, `front-office`, or `research`. Content types are concept, model, instrument, method, lab, research or market note.
